Self-initiated portfolio project

Survey & feedback sentiment dashboard

Tagged and visualized 300 real guest reviews by sentiment and theme — surfacing exactly where each hotel was winning or losing guests.

Problem
In my day-to-day work, manually coding sentiment and themes from open-text survey responses is slow and inconsistent across analysts. I wanted to test whether AI could safely accelerate that step.
User
Research operations analysts who need theme/sentiment reporting without a full manual coding cycle for every project.
What I built
A dashboard that ingests open-text responses and applies AI-based sentiment and theme tagging as a first pass, designed to layer onto standard dashboard delivery rather than replace it.
Design decision
Built it as an assisted first pass, not full automation — sentiment accuracy affects trust in research findings, so it needed a review step, not a black box.
Result
Manually tagging 300 reviews for sentiment/theme would typically take 3+ hours. This AI-assisted workflow completed it in under an hour — a ~70% time reduction, demonstrating how automation can be layered onto standard dashboard delivery.
Self-initiated portfolio project — built on Landbot

Lead generation chatbot for a market research company's website

Captured and qualified a real lead end-to-end — from first message to a sales-ready email notification, in under two minutes.

Problem
Manual handling of website inquiries led to inconsistent lead qualification and slow follow-up for a market research agency's sales team.
Users
Website visitors exploring the agency's services (market research, data analysis, chatbot development), and the sales team following up with them.
What I built
A conversational workflow that greets visitors, identifies which service they need, collects budget and timeline, and captures contact details — qualifying prospects through predefined business rules before handing structured leads to sales.
Design decision
Used rule-based qualification rather than open-ended conversation, so every captured lead arrives with the same structured fields (service interest, budget, timeline) — trading conversational flexibility for consistency sales can act on immediately.
Result
Reduced manual screening effort and standardized the information captured from incoming prospects. (Built and tested on Landbot's platform; the live demo is no longer hosted since the free tier period ended.)
Self-initiated portfolio project — built on Landbot

Customer satisfaction survey bot with conditional branching

Built a self-scoring feedback survey that routes respondents down a different follow-up path depending on their rating — automatically.

Problem
Standard satisfaction surveys ask the same follow-up questions regardless of the rating given, missing the chance to probe deeper on negative feedback specifically.
Users
Businesses collecting customer feedback, and the customers responding to the survey.
What I built
A conversational survey that asks for a star rating, then branches on logic (if rating < 3, route to "what can we improve" for detail; if rating ≥ 3, route to a recommend-likelihood question instead), logging every response to Google Sheets.
Design decision
Used a conditional branch rather than asking every respondent the same questions — low scores get a chance to explain why, without making happy respondents sit through irrelevant follow-ups.
Result
Every response logged automatically with no manual entry, and low-rating respondents routed to a targeted follow-up instead of a generic one. (Built and tested on Landbot's platform.)
